Your Move ring

Enter Apple Fitness’s red Move ring value: active calories burned, not the calorie goal or the Exercise ring minutes.

A daily Move total already includes workouts. When you enter or import it, workout calories stay in your log but are not added again. Exercise minutes do not add extra calories.

How calories out and net total work

Calories in = logged food. Calories out = resting energy + active energy. Resting energy comes from Apple Health/manual entry when available, otherwise your profile’s Mifflin–St Jeor estimate. Active energy comes from the daily Move total, or only your logged workouts if no Move total is entered. Without a daily total, ordinary walking and other unlogged movement are missing.

The net total is food minus calories out: a negative number suggests a deficit and a positive number a surplus. A profile estimate represents a whole day. Today’s ring may still be in progress, so today’s number is provisional. Your food goal already includes an activity assumption and does not automatically increase when workouts are added.

Workout estimates use net MET calories above resting, based on duration, effort and recorded/profile weight. Estimates and watch readings are approximate.

How the estimate works

Mifflin–St Jeor estimates resting energy using age, height, weight and sex. Activity estimates maintenance calories. Weekly pace adds or subtracts about 500 kcal/day per 1 lb/week.

Protein starts at 1.8 g/kg, fat at 25% of energy, and remaining calories go to carbs. These are adjustable starting estimates, not a promise of fat loss or muscle gain.

For adults 18+. The calculator limits deficits to 25% of maintenance and surpluses to 15%, with a 1,500 kcal male / 1,200 kcal female minimum. Track your weight trend for 2–3 weeks and adjust.

APPLE HEALTH IMPORT

Your rings, in your journal.

On your iPhone, open Health → your profile picture → Export All Health Data. Save the ZIP file, then choose it here. You can also select the unzipped export.xml file. This is a file import, not a live HealthKit connection.

The export is processed in your browser. Only selected activity totals and workout details are saved to your journal/account; unrelated health records and the full file are not uploaded.

Move ring daily summaries take priority when present. Otherwise only the selected source’s energy samples are used; records from different apps/devices are not summed together. Export sample totals may differ from Health’s own source-priority calculations.
