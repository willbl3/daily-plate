How the estimate works

Mifflin–St Jeor estimates resting energy using age, height, weight and sex. Activity estimates maintenance calories. Weekly pace adds or subtracts about 500 kcal/day per 1 lb/week.

Protein starts at 1.8 g/kg, fat at 25% of energy, and remaining calories go to carbs. These are adjustable starting estimates, not a promise of fat loss or muscle gain.

For adults 18+. The calculator limits deficits to 25% of maintenance and surpluses to 15%, with a 1,500 kcal male / 1,200 kcal female minimum. Track your weight trend for 2–3 weeks and adjust.
